**Full description**
```
Run your tuition or coaching classes from your phone — without notebooks, Excel or apps that upload your students' details to unknown servers.

FEES MADE EASY
• Monthly fees are created automatically for every student
• Collect a payment in seconds: cash, UPI or bank transfer
• Part payments and advance payments handled for you
• Waive a fee, add one-off charges like admission or books
• Instantly see who owes what, and since when

PDF RECEIPTS
• A neat receipt for every payment, with your institute name and UPI ID
• Share it on WhatsApp, email or print in one tap

WHATSAPP REMINDERS
• Send a polite fee reminder to a parent with the amount and months already written
• You check the message and tap Send — no SMS permission needed
• Optional morning notification when fees are overdue

ATTENDANCE
• Take a batch register in seconds: everyone starts present, tap the absentees
• Monthly attendance percentage on every student's page

DASHBOARD
• Today's classes, who is overdue, and this month's collection at a glance

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your data stays only on your phone, encrypted (AES-256)
• No sign-up, no ads, no tracking, no internet permission
• PIN or fingerprint lock
• Encrypted backup to Google Drive or anywhere you choose, locked with your own passphrase

IN ENGLISH AND HINDI
• Use the app in English or हिन्दी

FREE AND PRO
• Free: everything above for up to 15 students and 2 batches
• Pro: unlimited students and batches — pay once, no subscription

Made in India for home tutors, coaching centres, and music, dance, art and sports teachers.
```
