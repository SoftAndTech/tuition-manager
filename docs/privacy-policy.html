Privacy Policy

Tuition Manager (Android app) · Last updated: 23 October 2026

In short: Tuition Manager does not collect, upload or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your phone, encrypted. We (the developer) cannot see it.

Who we are

Tuition Manager is made by Soft And Tech Solutions, India. Contact: [support email].

What the app stores, and where

To help you run your tuition, the app stores what you type into it:

  • Students' names, classes and notes, and parents' names and phone numbers
  • Batches, fees, payments, receipts and attendance
  • Your own details for receipts (institute name, your name, phone, UPI ID)

All of this is stored only on your phone, in a database encrypted with AES-256 (SQLCipher). The encryption key is protected by your phone's Android Keystore. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send this data anywhere, and it is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive your students' details, your fee records, your usage or your location.

Backups

If you choose Create backup, the app writes one file, encrypted with a passphrase you choose, to a place you pick (for example Google Drive or a WhatsApp chat with yourself). That file is handled by the service you choose and is subject to its privacy policy. Without your passphrase nobody, including us, can open the file.

Sharing you start yourself

When you tap Remind, Share receipt or Call, the app opens WhatsApp, the share sheet or your phone dialer with a message or file you can review. Nothing is sent until you send it in that app. Those apps have their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Tuition Manager Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells the app (through the Play Store app on your phone) whether you own Pro. We do not receive your payment details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications, only if you turn on the daily overdue-fee reminder. It shows a count and total, never student names.
  • Fingerprint / face, only if you turn on fingerprint unlock. Your fingerprint never leaves your phone's secure hardware.
  • Run at startup / keep awake briefly, so the daily reminder still works after the phone restarts.
  • Google Play billing, to buy and check Pro.

The app does not ask for contacts, SMS, call logs, location, camera, microphone or storage access.

Children

The app is meant for tutors and coaching centres, not for children to use. It stores students' details only on the tutor's own phone, under the tutor's control.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Security & app lock → Erase all data, or uninstall the app. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, this deletes everything. Backup files you saved elsewhere can be deleted from wherever you saved them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
